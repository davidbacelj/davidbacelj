# davidbacelj.com

David Bacelj's personal website. Astro 7, static, deployed on Vercel from this public repository. The repo is named after the GitHub account, so `README.md` is also David's GitHub profile page: keep it a short intro, and put developer notes in `DEVELOPMENT.md`.

## Rules

- The repo is open source. Never commit secrets, `.env` files, private notes or personal data that isn't already meant to be public. Never publish David's home address, phone number or any other private email. His Gmail (`PERSON.email` in `profile.ts`) is public on purpose, in Get in touch, `llms.txt` and the structured data (his call, 2026-10-23).
- Every fact about David lives in `src/data/` and must come from a public source or from David himself. Never invent numbers, quotes, dates, awards or roles.
- No trackers, cookies, analytics or third-party requests. Fonts are self-hosted through `@fontsource`. Visitors get no JavaScript unless a feature needs it. The one exception is a news clip in another site's player (`Clip.astro`, 2026-10-23: SVT's FossilEye clip), which loads only when the visitor presses its button. Its origin is the only `frame-src` in `astro.config.mjs`. Never re-host someone else's video.
- Security: Astro writes a hashed CSP into each page (`astro.config.mjs`); the other headers are in `vercel.json`. Keep both when adding anything.
- Pin exact dependency versions in `package.json`.
- Copy follows the writing rules: plain, specific, first person, no em or en dashes, sentence case headings.
- `/llms.txt` (2026-10-22) describes David for AI assistants, in the third person, and brags as hard as the facts allow (he asked for it to lift him up as a top candidate; invented claims still stay out). Its hand-written parts are in `src/data/llms.ts` and must change when a story they draw on changes; the list of posts and the sources are built from the road in `src/pages/llms.txt.ts`.
- Never push, deploy or publish without David's explicit go.

## Design rulebook

The site (picked by David 2026-10-20, after many rounds of concepts) is "Stage", built in `src/components/LookStage.astro` (the home page) and `src/components/StagePost.astro` (one post per project at `/story/<id>`):

- His tagline everywhere (2026-10-26, his pick of three directions): "Engineer who sells." It is `PERSON.tagline` under his name on the first screen, opens the Google description (`PERSON.description`) and the `llms.txt` summary, and he uses it on LinkedIn and X too.
- The first screen is the stage photo of him in the black suit at the podium (`PHOTOS.stage`, his own 3500 px original), full bleed, with his name huge over it, one line under it, and simple round icons for LinkedIn, X and GitHub. Straight under the photo comes the welcome film (2026-10-20: he removed the big numbers too; a credentials grid and labelled logo rows there had "looked very bad").
- Then a big welcome film at the start of the road. Until he records it, the frame shows his sofa photo (`PHOTOS.sofa`), a play button and "Coming soon". Under it, his script for the film (version A, his notes in order, picked 2026-10-21) as text in a white card (`src/data/welcome.ts`); "close to three million dollars" is his own wording for the 26.5M SEK round. Its "get in touch" links to the contact section (`#contact`).
- Then the road: one thin ink line straight down the middle, drawing itself as you scroll (the line from concept D; a wide road band was rejected), with a small dot on it above each post. It is NOT by year: the posts and personal photos come in David's own order, set in `src/data/road.ts` (the build fails if one is missing or doubled), and a post's Previous and Next follow that order. Each project is one plain card centred on the line (no left/right, no stack of paper), led by a big photo of him. His picked photos lead their cards (`src/data/covers.ts`); the rest use their section's photo. A card shows the date and role, a reading time, the headline (`src/data/headlines.ts`), one line, its bottom line from `src/data/highlights.ts` (a big value, then a short label: a number or his role, like "CTO of RoboWaste" and "Head of AI vision at Wheelscanning", 2026-10-23; each one adds what the headline doesn't already say; 2026-10-26 the value is drawn with a yellow highlighter pen, `.marker` in `base.css`, which draws in as the card scrolls into view and once at the top of the post), and "Read the post". The road's wrapper and cards use `overflow: clip`, not `hidden`, or the scroll-driven marker stops following the page. No logos on the cards (2026-10-22: they belong in the post), except that a post that opens with its logos (`logoCover`) shows the same logo tiles as its card's picture (FossilEye, 2026-10-23). What he rejected, so it is not tried again: grids and bentos ("messy"), a drawing sheet, detection boxes, a quiet serif editorial, a macOS desktop, a launch page, a fully dark cinematic page, widgets, a chat, a photo album, paved, forest or wide roads, stations, storybook chapters, a horizontal road, a slide deck, a Mario-like game, a brainstorm map, the cork board and post-its ("too playful"), a stack-of-paper card, a PDF-like reader, pixel-art placeholders, and credentials or logo rows under the hero. Concepts tried and set aside on 2026-10-20 (in git history): Keynote, Monograph, Track record, Route, Exhibition, Press kit, Scroll story, CV, Collage (after a colleague's site he liked; take its grammar, never copy it), Documents. The collage may come back at the bottom of the page later.

- He wants many real images: photos of him and his work, product screens and logos. Use ALL his photos (`src/assets/life/` came from his camera; their metadata, including GPS, is stripped on import, and must stay stripped). Videos go to `public/media/`, re-encoded with every metadata field stripped.
- The content lives in `src/data/sections.ts` (stops, sections, photos, logos, links, film), with the written stories in `stories.ts`, the captions in `captions.ts`, the card lines (numbers or roles) in `highlights.ts`, the headlines in `headlines.ts` and the intro and links in `profile.ts`. `documents.ts` splits a story into its parts and photos for a post.
- Logos: `Logo.astro` sets the height by class (the CSP blocks style attributes). In a post, the logos sit in `LogoWall.astro` (2026-10-22, he wanted them bigger, in a standard container, placed naturally in the article): one white tile per logo (dark tile for a white-only logo), the same tile size in every post, three a row (two for two or four logos, three and two for five), each logo sized by area so a square mark and a long wordmark look equally big, with "Together with ..." under them like a photo caption. They go at the end of the story's first section, before its second small heading. A logo that still looks small gets `scale` in `sections.ts` (Toyota's stacked logo has 1.3). One-colour versions of every logo are in `src/assets/logos/mono/` (`monoLogos.ts`); nothing uses them since the cards lost their logos.
- Icons come from Simple Icons (CC0) through `Icon.astro`; only tools David has used.
